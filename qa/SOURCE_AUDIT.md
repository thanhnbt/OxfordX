Reading recaps and listening practice are short authored adaptations; complete poems/articles, original audio recordings and all workbook tasks are not included.

Manual source checks:

- Bella's journey is imaginary; she ends back in her bedroom. Apparent size changes with distance; source PDF 38–40.
- The Earth/Venus comparison uses the book's claims: almost the same size, iron and rock cores, Venus hotter with thicker clouds; source PDF 46, 48.
- Hidden Army facts use material, differences in faces/uniforms, ranks and paint preservation; source PDF 58–60. The unopened-tomb statement is framed as a claim in the reading, not a present-day update.
- Listening adaptations preserve reasons/main idea and numbers/similarities and differences. A light-year is correctly described as a distance; only the approximate Sun–Earth light time is retained from the listening script.
- Child text uses short definitions and sentence frames. Teacher/source diagnostics are in the grown-up modal. Open writing/speaking receives no false automatic grading.

Visual review:

Pronunciation: learning IPA and sound-out manually reviewed for target, plural endings and primary stress. The following difficult forms were compared with official Cambridge pronunciation information:

- [observatory](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/us/pronunciation/english/observatory)
- [archaeologist](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/us/pronunciation/english/archaeologist)
